# Verdicts

The owner's judgement calls on real cases, one per line, newest last. **Read
this before judging anything.** Where a line here disagrees with the prose in
another rules file, the line wins — the prose is what we thought the rule was;
the verdict is what the owner decided about a case in front of them.

Append-only. A wrong verdict gets a later line that says so; nothing here is
edited. Written by `deploy/verdict.mjs`, in the same turn it is said.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD | scope | the call | why`

Scopes: mail, message, people, decision, calendar, brief, ui, general.
